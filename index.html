Get better at poker and blackjack.

Card Coach is a training app for iPhone, iPad, Android and the web. Play against computer opponents with distinct styles while a coach shows you the math behind every decision, then review your session to see exactly where to improve.

Play in your browser

♠ Hold'em Coach

No-limit Texas Hold'em against five playing styles. Hints show your equity, pot odds, and a recommended play; the coach reads your opponents and explains position and hand strength.

♦ Blackjack Coach

See the exact expected value of hit, stand, double, split, and surrender. Learn basic strategy from a chart built for your table's rules, and practice Hi-Lo card counting.

Built for learning

  • Every hint and explanation is calculated on your device — the app works fully offline.
  • Session reviews separate luck from skill and point to your costliest mistakes.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Card Coach uses play chips with no cash value. There is no real-money gambling, and nothing can be won or purchased. It's intended for adults aged 18+.
